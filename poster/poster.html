Measuring the performance loss of AI resource allocation in 5G networks
Mark Zhao & Stanley FindsenSupervisor: Dev SinghUniversity of Auckland

1The problem

Every millisecond, a 5G base station must share its limited radio capacity between three types of task:

eMBBvideo and downloads URLLCrobots and cars mMTCsensor readings

Reinforcement learning (RL) is widely proposed to optimise this decision.

6Conclusions

  • An AI trained on balanced traffic performs as well as one retrained for each environment, so there is no need to train a new model every time the traffic changes.
  • Balanced agent transfers well across different traffic conditions.
